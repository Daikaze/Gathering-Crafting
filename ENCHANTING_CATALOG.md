# Enchanting Reference — Gathering & Crafting v1.3.0

The first 25 enchantments preserve their original GP prices and time requirements. Additional formulas are homebrew.
Each enchantment is restricted to the named equipment classes and requires its own learned formula.

## Profession ranks

| Rank | Title | Training days | Training cost | Arcana DC |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | Novice | 3 | 50 gp | 10 |
| 2 | Apprentice | 5 | 150 gp | 12 |
| 3 | Adept | 7 | 400 gp | 14 |
| 4 | Expert | 10 | 900 gp | 16 |
| 5 | Master | 14 | 2000 gp | 18 |

Players begin Untrained. Training requires a physical Jeweler's Tools or Enchanter's Tools item.
After the indicated campaign days, the player makes an Arcana check to finish; failure postpones the next attempt by one world day (no extra tuition).
Each formula is studied individually for 15% of its enchantment cost (minimum 20 gp, rounded to 5 gp), plus 2–6 in-world study days, and an Arcana check.
A character must know the formula and have a high-enough Enchanting rank before starting an enchantment.

## Item capacity

- Common: 1 enchantment slot, maximum recipe Rank 2.
- Uncommon: 2 slots, maximum Rank 3.
- Rare: 3 slots, maximum Rank 4.
- Very Rare: 4 slots, maximum Rank 5.
- Legendary / Artifact: 5 slots, maximum Rank 5.
- GM can override item capacity to 0–6 slots from the Enchanting tab.
- Stronger recipes explicitly require 2 slots. Duplicate, conflicting and pending enchantments are disallowed.
- Enchantments apply only to weapon/armor types classified by D&D5e data: bow, crossbow, blade, staff, polearm, bludgeoning, piercing, melee, ranged, light/medium/heavy armor, shield.
- Existing v1.2 enchanting jobs are allowed to complete without retroactive new slot restrictions.

## Automation

Gold charges, training, world time, recipe knowledge, compatibility, capacity, selected elemental damage, magic +1/+2, and once-per-Long-Rest charges are automated.
Passive skill bonuses are placed as item Active Effects when the Foundry item permits that operation; equip-state behavior should be checked in a live D&D5e 5.3.3 world.
Powers can be activated from the Enchanting window or the actor sheet. They record usage and send a chat card, but do not automatically choose targets or resolve spells, movement, resistance, reactions, or complex combat consequences. These remain GM adjudicated.
