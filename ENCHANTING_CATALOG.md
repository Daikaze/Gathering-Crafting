# Enchanting Reference — Gathering & Crafting v1.3.2

All 93 Enchantments are available by learning their formulas. **Gold is an estimated market price / service value**, not a crafting material. Using the Enchanting tab never deducts coins from the maker, charges training tuition, or automatically transfers GP to a merchant. The game master may roleplay customer payments separately.

## Learn Enchanting (without tuition)

Players start Untrained and need a physical **Jeweler's Tools** or **Enchanter's Tools** item. The ranks are trained sequentially. After the listed world days, make an Arcana check. A failed check delays another attempt by one day. Only the GM sees the target DC in the interface; chat sends target-DC details privately to the GM.

| Rank | Title | Training days | Arcana DC (GM only) |
|---:|---|---:|---:|
| 1 | Novice | 2 | 10 |
| 2 | Apprentice | 4 | 12 |
| 3 | Adept | 5 | 14 |
| 4 | Expert | 7 | 16 |
| 5 | Master | 10 | 18 |

Each Enchantment formula must be studied separately; it takes **1–4 world days**, depending on the required proficiency rank, and another Arcana check, but **no gold**. The formula study time is Rank 1: 2 day(s), Rank 2: 2 day(s), Rank 3: 3 day(s), Rank 4: 4 day(s), Rank 5: 4 day(s).

## Item limits

- Common: 1 enchantment slot, maximum formula Rank 2.
- Uncommon: 2 slots, maximum Rank 3.
- Rare: 3 slots, maximum Rank 4.
- Very Rare: 4 slots, maximum Rank 5.
- Legendary / Artifact: 5 slots, maximum Rank 5.
- GM may override item capacity to 0–6 slots from the Enchanting tab.
- Selected elite formulas require 2 slots. Duplicates, mutual exclusions, and another pending job on the same item remain blocked.
- Weapons: melee, ranged, blade, bow, crossbow, staff, polearm, bludgeoning and piercing tags. Armor: light, medium, heavy and shields.
- A pending enchantment created before this version retains its original `finishAt` world timestamp; shortened durations apply to **new** jobs. Earlier spending is not retroactively refunded.

## DC visibility and automated behavior

Target DCs are displayed only to GMs in the Gathering, Alchemy, Blacksmithing and Enchanting interfaces, and detailed check messages are sent as GM whispers. Players see outcomes and their check totals without the target DC. Item names, market values, durations and compatibility remain visible to players.

Enchanting uses Foundry **world time** (one day = 86,400 world-time seconds), but never advances it automatically. Native +1/+2 item bonus, selected elemental damage and supported Active Effects can be written on the Item. Enchantment power Use buttons track once-per-Long-Rest uses. Advanced spells, reactions and conditional effects still require GM adjudication.
